# Building Permit Analysis Findings (2020-2025)

## Executive Summary

Analysis of Philadelphia building permits (2020-2025) reveals a dramatic shift in multifamily development patterns compared to DVRPC's 2007-2017 baseline. Recent multifamily construction is **significantly less concentrated** around BSL/MFL rapid transit stations.

## Key Findings

### 1. Overall Development Activity

- **Total building permits (2020-2025)**: 8,523 (excluding plumbing/electrical/mechanical)
- **Multifamily permits identified**: 202 (2.4% of all building permits)
- **Average units per permit**: 1.6 units

### 2. Spatial Distribution Relative to BSL/MFL Stations

| Distance from Station | Permits | Percentage | Implication |
|----------------------|---------|------------|-------------|
| Within 0.25 miles    | 26      | 12.9%      | Immediate station area |
| Within 0.5 miles     | 87      | 43.1%      | Standard TOD zone |
| Within 1.0 miles     | 167     | 82.7%      | Greater transit corridor |

**Key Insight**: Only 13% of recent multifamily permits are within a quarter-mile of BSL/MFL stations, compared to DVRPC's finding of 8,520 units in the same areas during 2007-2017.

### 3. Comparison to DVRPC Baseline (2007-2017)

| Metric | 2007-2017 (DVRPC) | 2020-2025 (Current) | Change |
|--------|------------------|---------------------|--------|

## Interpretation

### What This Means for TOC Policy

1. **Development Has Decentralized**: The 99% decrease in station-adjacent development suggests multifamily construction is happening throughout Philadelphia, not concentrated at rapid transit nodes.

2. **0.25-Mile Radius May Be Too Restrictive**: 43% of multifamily permits are within 0.5 miles of stations, suggesting DVRPC may have used this larger (and more standard) TOD radius.

3. **BSL/MFL Alone Misses Recent Activity**: Current development patterns may favor:
   - Trolley corridors (Routes 10, 11, 13, 15, 34, 36)
   - High-frequency bus routes (29 routes in Bus Revolution network)
   - Areas between rapid transit lines

4. **Political Implications by District**:
   - **District 7** has most active stations (York-Dauphin, Berks, Huntingdon)
   - **Districts 2 and 5** have moderate activity
   - **Districts 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9** have very limited BSL/MFL-adjacent development

## Recommendations

### For Policy Design

1. **Expand Geographic Scope**:
   - Use 0.5-mile radius (standard TOD buffer) instead of 0.25 miles
   - Include trolley corridors and high-frequency bus routes
   - Consider "transit-rich areas" vs. single-station focus

2. **Adjust Development Expectations**:
   - Current data shows ~50 units/5 years near BSL/MFL stations
   - DVRPC showed ~850 units/year (8,520 over 10 years)
   - TOC policy may not trigger significant new development at existing BSL/MFL nodes

3. **Alternative Zoning Approach**:
   - Focus on **preserving affordability** at existing multifamily near transit
   - Target **underutilized** station areas for future growth incentives
   - Acknowledge development is market-driven and dispersed

### For Political Messaging

**Honest Assessment**:
> "DVRPC's 2017 analysis showed robust development near BSL and MFL stations from 2007-2017, with over 8,500 new units. Our analysis of 2020-2025 permits shows only 49 units in the same areas - a 99% decrease. This doesn't mean development stopped; it means it's happening elsewhere in the city. TOC policy should reflect where housing IS being built, not just where rapid transit exists."

**Trolley Corridor Emphasis**:
> "Philadelphia's trolley network and emerging high-frequency bus network serve far more neighborhoods than BSL/MFL alone. If TOC policy aims to promote transit-oriented development, it should include these modes where we see active multifamily construction today."

## Methodology Notes

### Data Sources
- **Building Permits**: Philadelphia L&I OpenDataPhilly, 2020-2025
- **Transit Stations**: DVRPC TOD Evaluation Tool (30 BSL/MFL stations)
- **Baseline Comparison**: DVRPC "Building on Our Strengths" (2017)

### Filters Applied
- **Building permits only**: Excluded plumbing (PP-*), electrical (EP-*), mechanical (MP-*), fire (FP-*), sprinkler (SP-*) permits
- **Multifamily identification**: Keywords including "two family," "apartment," "duplex," "multifamily," "X dwelling units," 4+ story buildings
- **Deduplication**: Same address counted once (multiple permits for same building)
- **Unit extraction**: Parsed permit scopes for unit counts (e.g., "TWO FAMILY" = 2 units)

### Limitations
- Unit counts are estimates based on permit language
- Some multifamily projects may use single-family language
- Permit issuance ≠ construction completion
- Does not account for demolitions or net change
- Geographic scope limited to BSL/MFL; trolleys not yet analyzed
