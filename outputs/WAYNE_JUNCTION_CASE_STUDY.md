## II. THE INDUSTRIAL ZONING PROBLEM

### **Why Variances Are Required**

Wayne Junction area is predominantly zoned **I-2 (General Industrial)** and **I-1 (Light Industrial)**, which:
- **Prohibit residential use** as primary use category
- **Require variances** from Zoning Board of Adjustment (ZBA) for any multifamily
- **Give council member veto power** through ZBA process and community input

### **The Variance Process Barrier**

**Step 1: ZBA Application**
- $1,000+ application fee
- Detailed plans, surveys, legal descriptions
- 60-90 day process minimum

**Step 2: Community Notification**
- Notices to neighbors within 250 feet
- Community meetings (where CM Bass can mobilize opposition)
- RCO (Registered Community Organization) review

**Step 3: ZBA Hearing**
- Legal representation required ($5,000-$15,000)
- CM Bass testimony carries significant weight with ZBA
- Neighbors can object (often organized by council office)

**Step 4: Appeals**
- If denied, appeal to Court of Common Pleas ($10,000-$25,000 legal fees)
- 6-12 month process
- Uncertain outcome

**TOTAL COST & RISK:**
- **$15,000-$40,000** in variance costs per project
- **6-18 months** added timeline
- **High political risk** if council member is hostile

**Ken Weinstein's Problem:** CM Bass can effectively block his projects at any of these steps, even if they meet zoning criteria, simply by mobilizing neighborhood opposition or testifying against variances.
